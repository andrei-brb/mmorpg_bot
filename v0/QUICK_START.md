# 🎮 Quick Start Guide - Your First Adventure!

## 🎯 Step-by-Step First Session:

### 1️⃣ Create Your Character
```
/character create
```
- Choose a class that matches your playstyle
- Warrior = Tank, Mage = DPS, Priest = Healer, etc.

### 2️⃣ Check Your Stats
```
/character profile
```
See your starting stats, HP, and equipment slots.

### 3️⃣ Explore Your Zone
```
/explore
```
- Gives you XP and gold
- Can find items
- Has a 30-second cooldown
- Run this 5-10 times to level up!

### 4️⃣ Enter Combat
```
/fight
```
- Turn-based combat system
- Use abilities to defeat enemies
- Gain XP and loot when you win!

### 5️⃣ Manage Your Inventory
```
/inventory
```
- View all your items
- `/equip <id>` - Equip better gear
- `/sell <id>` - Sell items for gold

### 6️⃣ Level Up & Specialize
- At level 10, use `/character specialize` to choose your specialization
- This unlocks new abilities and changes your playstyle!

## 🗺️ Available Zones:

- **Elwynn Forest** (Levels 1-10) - Starting zone
- **Dun Morogh** (Levels 1-10) - Dwarven mountains
- **The Barrens** (Levels 10-25) - Desert wasteland
- **Stranglethorn Vale** (Levels 25-45) - Dangerous jungle
- **Blackrock Depths** (Levels 50-60) - Endgame dungeon

Use `/travel <zone_name>` to move between zones!

---

## 💡 Pro Tips:

1. **Explore often** - It's the fastest way to level up early
2. **Fight bosses** - They drop rare loot and lots of XP
3. **Join a guild** - `/guild create` to start your own or join others
4. **Check daily quests** - `/daily` for bonus rewards
5. **Watch for world events** - `/events` shows active bonuses
